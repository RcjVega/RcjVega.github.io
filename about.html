Ray Carlos Junior Vega Lugo

I am an economist, researcher and Lecturer at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos. I hold a Master’s degree in Economics from Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú and I am an economist from Universidad Nacional del Callao.

My work focuses on development economics, infrastructure economics, impact evaluation, regulation and competition policy, applied microeconometrics and public policy.
