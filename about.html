Academic Profile

Ray Carlos Junior Vega Lugo

I am an economist, researcher and Lecturer at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos. I hold a Master's degree in Economics from Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú.

My work is centered on applied economic research, with interests in development economics, infrastructure economics, impact evaluation, regulation and competition policy, applied microeconometrics and public policy.

Research

Economics, infrastructure and socioeconomic outcomes

My research examines how infrastructure investment, access to public services, natural resources and regulatory institutions shape poverty, welfare and regional development. I am particularly interested in empirical strategies that connect rigorous causal analysis with policy-relevant questions in Peru and Latin America.

My empirical work uses panel-data methods, dynamic panel estimators and quasi-experimental approaches to study heterogeneous effects across regions, sectors and population groups.

Teaching

Connecting economic theory with evidence

As a Lecturer at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos, I teach and develop materials in Industrial Organization and related areas of Microeconomics and Econometrics. My teaching emphasizes formal economic reasoning, empirical evidence and applications to real markets and public policy.
