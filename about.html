Ray Carlos Junior Vega Lugo

I am an economist, researcher and Lecturer at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos (UNMSM).

I hold a Master's degree in Economics from Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP). My research interests include Industrial Organization, Infrastructure Economics, Competition Policy, Poverty and Inequality, Transport Economics, and Applied Econometrics.

Teaching

As a Lecturer at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos, my teaching interests include Industrial Organization, Microeconomics and Applied Econometrics. I develop course materials that connect economic theory, empirical methods and applications to real markets and public policy.

Research Profile

My research studies the effects of public-service infrastructure investment on total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions. My empirical work combines regional panel data with static and dynamic econometric models, including pooled estimators, random effects, fixed effects, Arellano–Bond estimators and GMM methods.
