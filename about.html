Ray Carlos Junior Vega Lugo

I am an economist working at the intersection of applied economics, economic regulation and public policy. My research interests include industrial organization, infrastructure economics, poverty and inequality, competition policy, transport economics and applied econometrics.

I currently work at the Urban Transport Authority for Lima and Callao (ATU), where my professional interests include economic regulation, financial and economic information, regulatory accounting and the design of evidence-based regulatory tools for urban transport.

Academic Profile

I am a Master's candidate in Economics at Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC). My current research studies the effects of public-service infrastructure investment on total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions.

The research combines regional panel data with static and dynamic econometric models, including pooled estimators, random effects, fixed effects and dynamic panel methods.

Research Interests

  • Industrial Organization
  • Infrastructure Economics
  • Poverty and Inequality
  • Competition Policy
  • Transport Economics and Regulation
  • Applied Econometrics and Panel Data

Research Approach

My work emphasizes empirical analysis, transparent quantitative methods and policy relevance. I am particularly interested in research that connects rigorous econometric evidence with practical questions in infrastructure provision, market regulation and public policy.
