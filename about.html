Ray Carlos Junior Vega Lugo

I am an economist, researcher and lecturer working at the intersection of applied economics, industrial organization and public policy. My research interests include industrial organization, infrastructure economics, poverty and inequality, competition policy, transport economics and applied econometrics.

I am a Lecturer at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos, where my teaching interests include Industrial Organization, Microeconomics and Applied Econometrics.

Academic Profile

I hold a Master's degree in Economics from Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP). My research studies the effects of public-service infrastructure investment on total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions.

My empirical work combines regional panel data with static and dynamic econometric models, including pooled estimators, random effects, fixed effects, Arellano–Bond estimators and GMM methods.

Research Interests

  • Industrial Organization
  • Infrastructure Economics
  • Poverty and Inequality
  • Competition Policy
  • Transport Economics and Regulation
  • Applied Econometrics and Panel Data

Research and Teaching

My work emphasizes empirical analysis, transparent quantitative methods and policy relevance. I am particularly interested in connecting rigorous economic analysis with practical questions in infrastructure provision, market regulation, competition and public policy.
