Research Profile

Applied research at the intersection of infrastructure, development and regulation

I am an economist and researcher studying how infrastructure investment, regulation and public policy shape poverty, welfare and regional economic performance. My work combines applied microeconomics, impact evaluation and panel-data econometrics, with a particular focus on Peru and Latin America.

Current Work

Selected Research

Infrastructure Investment and Poverty in Peru

Research on how public-service infrastructure investment affects total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions, using static and dynamic panel estimators.

Water, Sanitation and Human Capital

Quasi-experimental research on the socioeconomic and human-capital effects of expanding access to water and sanitation infrastructure.

Natural Resources and Local Development

Applied research on mining, poverty and regional development, with attention to causal identification and heterogeneous local effects.
