Research Profile

I am an economist and researcher interested in how infrastructure investment, market structure, regulation and public policy affect socioeconomic outcomes. My work combines applied microeconomics, panel-data econometrics and policy analysis, with a particular focus on Peru and Latin America.

Featured Research

Infrastructure Investment and Poverty in Peru

Empirical research on the effects of public-service infrastructure investment on total and extreme poverty, using regional panel data and static and dynamic panel estimators.

Economic Regulation and Urban Transport

Applied work on economic regulation, regulatory accounting, financial information and evidence-based decision-making for public transport systems.

Industrial Organization and Competition Policy

Research and teaching interests in market power, strategic interaction, mergers, patents, innovation incentives and competition policy.
