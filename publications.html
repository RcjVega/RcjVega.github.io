Effects of Infrastructure Investment on Poverty in Peru

Empirical research examining the relationship between investment in public-service infrastructure and total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions. The study uses regional panel data and compares pooled, random-effects, fixed-effects and dynamic-panel estimators.
