My research focuses on infrastructure, poverty, development economics, regulation and applied microeconometrics. This page presents current working papers and ongoing projects. Preprints and supplementary materials will be added as they become publicly available.

Working Paper

Effects of Infrastructure Investment on Poverty in Peru

Ray Carlos Junior Vega Lugo

This paper studies the relationship between investment in public-service infrastructure and total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions. The empirical strategy compares pooled, random-effects and fixed-effects specifications with dynamic panel estimators, allowing the analysis to account for poverty persistence and potential endogeneity.
