Research Agenda

Understanding how infrastructure and institutions shape development

My research examines how infrastructure investment, access to public services, natural resources and regulatory institutions affect poverty, welfare and regional economic performance. I combine applied microeconomics with panel-data methods and quasi-experimental strategies, with a primary focus on Peru and Latin America.

Working Papers

Effects of Infrastructure Investment on Poverty in Peru

This paper studies how public-service infrastructure investment affects total and extreme poverty across Peruvian regions using regional panel data and dynamic panel estimators.
