Teaching Profile

Economics education grounded in theory and evidence

I teach economics at Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos. My teaching approach connects formal economic models with empirical evidence, real-world markets and current public-policy questions.

Current Teaching

Industrial Organization

The course studies firm behavior and market performance under imperfect competition. Students work with economic models and applied cases to understand strategic interaction, market power and competition policy.

Market PowerCournot CompetitionBertrand CompetitionStackelberg ModelsProduct DifferentiationMergersInnovation & PatentsCompetition Policy
Additional Teaching Areas

Microeconomics

Consumer and producer theory, market equilibrium, welfare analysis, uncertainty and strategic behavior.

Econometrics

Regression analysis, causal inference, panel data and applied empirical methods for economic research.

Public Economics

Market failures, taxation, public expenditure, redistribution and the economic analysis of government intervention.
